# Guided-modes-of-dielectric-slab-waveguide
An quasi-analytical approach for examining the guided modes of a dielectric slab waveguide composed of thin film lithium niobate (TFLN) or lithium niobate on insulator (LNOI) media.

## Abstract: 
This project report presents an analytical approach for examining the guided modes of a dielectric slab waveguide
composed of thin film lithium niobate (TFLN) or lithium niobate on insulator (LNOI) media. The investigation starts
with the determination of the effective indices of the guided mode and the examination of field profiles in an isotropic
waveguide, defined in terms of refractive indices, layer thickness, and a given vacuum wavelength. Subsequently, two
distinct configurations of LN material, namely X-cut and Z-cut, are considered. In the X-cut configuration, strong
mode hybridization is observed for near-degenerate modes in the vertically non-symmetric configuration and for all
the other modes, mildly hybridization is observed with clear TE and TM dominant polarization. Effective indices of
the guided modes for X-cut configuration depend on the propagation direction relative to the crystal axis whereas in
the Z-cut configuration effective indices of the guided modes are direction independent and TE and TM polarization
can be clearly distinguished by two separate uncoupled equations. This report illustrates the dependence of effective
indices of the guided modes on waveguide thickness and propagation angle at a specific wavelength and discusses the
vectorial field profiles along with their characterization of polarization.
